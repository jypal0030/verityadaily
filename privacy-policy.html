b) Information collected automatically

2. How we use your information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site.
  • To send our newsletter (only if you subscribe — you can unsubscribe anytime).

GDPR (European Economic Area): Consent (for cookies, newsletter, non-essential tracking), Legitimate interests (basic site operations, security), Contract (responding to your requests).
