Apple Event September 9 Confirmed: “Surprise and Shine” — iPhone 18 Pro, First Foldable, Watch 12

Apple has officially confirmed its September event — and the teaser says “Surprise and Shine.” The keynote lands on Wednesday, September 9, 2026 at 10 a.m. Pacific (1 p.m. ET, 10:30 PM IST), per AppleInsider and Forbes. The “surprise” everyone expects: Apple’s first-ever foldable iPhone, reportedly launching alongside the iPhone 18 Pro lineup. Twelve days out, and the rumor mill is already at full speed.

When and How to Watch (India Time)

  • Date: Wednesday, September 9, 2026 — officially confirmed
  • Time: 10 a.m. Pacific / 1 p.m. Eastern — that’s 10:30 PM IST for India
  • Where: Apple.com and Apple’s official YouTube channel (typical live stream setup)
  • Pre-orders: historically the Friday after — expect September 11, with in-store availability the following week (CNET is tracking preorder timing)

What’s Expected: The Full Lineup

iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max — the headline hardware. MacRumors’ roundup points to the A20 Pro chip generation and camera upgrades as the marquee Pro changes. Apple’s teaser wording “Shine” has analysts reading into display or design refreshes, but Apple hasn’t confirmed hardware details beyond the event itself.

The “Surprise”: iPhone Fold / “Ultra” — the foldable everyone’s waiting for. MacRumors reports Apple plans to introduce its first foldable iPhone in September 2026, launching alongside the iPhone 18 Pro models. Tom’s Guide’s leak roundup claims dual rear 48MP cameras (no telephoto), Touch ID, and a 4,883 mAh battery — treat those as leaks until Apple confirms on stage. CNET notes Apple “has neither confirmed the iPhone Fold nor announced a release date,” which is exactly why the word “Surprise” is doing heavy lifting in that teaser.

Apple Watch 12 and the usual software release timing round out the expected slate — Tom’s Guide’s event preview includes Watch 12 among the announced products expected.

The Fold Is the Real Story — Here’s Why

Apple has watched Samsung, Google, and Chinese brands own the foldable category for years without entering. If the September 9 event confirms a foldable iPhone, it validates the category overnight — and immediately raises the stakes for every competitor. Leaks suggest a crease-free display goal and premium pricing well above the Pro Max. For India — one of Apple’s fastest-growing markets — pricing and launch-day availability will decide whether the Fold is a niche halo product or a genuine volume play here.

What Happens Next

Between now and September 9: expect invite press coverage to intensify, final leak waves (colors, pricing bands, carrier deals), and Apple’s traditional last-minute teaser videos. On event day: four or five product announcements, a software release-date announcement, and pre-orders opening that Friday. This page will be updated with everything Apple confirms on stage — bookmark it for the live rundown.

Bottom line: September 9, 10:30 PM IST. The iPhone 18 Pro is the certain bet; the foldable iPhone is the “Surprise” Apple is teasing. Pre-orders likely Friday, September 11. We’ll update this page live during the keynote.
