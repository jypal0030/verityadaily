GTA 6 “An Extended Look” Premieres Tonight on Netflix: India Time, Free YouTube Option, What to Expect

Share
UPDATE (August 28 morning): The footage has now aired. The Netflix premiere ran overnight, and the free YouTube release followed on Rockstar’s channel this morning. Confirmed highlights: Lucia in a playable dirt-bike race on a dedicated race track, a dress-picking interaction between Lucia and Jason that set the internet on fire, and extended open-world sequences across Leonida — details Mashable counted as “12 details not seen in” the earlier trailers. Media previews are now rolling out per the embargo that followed. We’ll keep this page updated as the reaction wave settles.

Tonight is the closest GTA 6 has ever been to playable. Rockstar Games premieres “Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look” tonight — Thursday, August 27 at 3 p.m. ET (12:30 AM IST, Friday) — and in a franchise first, the premiere is Netflix-first, with the free YouTube release following hours later. This is the biggest entertainment event of the year for search, and the first time a game reveal has skipped the usual live-stream circus for a streaming platform.

When and Where to Watch (India Times)

  • Netflix premiere: 12:30 AM IST, Friday, August 28 (3 p.m. ET Thursday) — available to Netflix members, per Rockstar’s Newswire and Netflix Tudum
  • Free YouTube release: Friday morning — IGN’s global-times listing shows 9 a.m. Friday in one listed timezone (6:30 AM IST equivalent window); check Rockstar’s channel for the exact drop
  • Also on: the official GTA VI site simultaneously with YouTube

A Netflix subscription is the only way to see it at 12:30 AM IST. Everyone else — that’s most of India — gets it on YouTube in the morning. Rockstar announced the Netflix partnership on August 6, and Netflix Tudum confirms: “Netflix members get an early look at the hotly anticipated game Aug. 27 at 3 p.m. ET.”

Why the Netflix-First Premiere Actually Matters

Game reveals have always been free, live and loud — trailer drops on YouTube, state-of-play streams, E3-stage chaos. Putting the first extended look of the most anticipated game of the decade behind a streaming login is a genuine first, and it signals where Rockstar’s marketing is headed: the GTA VI audience is being treated like a Netflix audience — the same one that made Squid Game a global event. For Rockstar’s parent Take-Two, it’s also a splashy way to deepen the Netflix partnership announced earlier this decade for game adaptations. For viewers, the trade is simple: pay nothing but wait six hours, or log in at midnight and see it first.

What “An Extended Look” Might Show

Rockstar hasn’t published the runtime — an unconfirmed Reddit claim suggests around 20 minutes, but that’s not official, so treat it as rumor. What’s confirmed: this follows the two official trailers (Trailer 1 in December 2023, Trailer 2 in May 2025), and expectations center on extended gameplay, the state of Leonida’s world, and dual protagonists Lucia and Jason. The game launches November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S — Mashable confirms “PC players will have to wait,” with no PC date announced. Expect the reveal to end on that date card one more time.

The India Angle: A 6 AM Friday Wake-Up Call

For India — now one of the loudest GTA fandoms on earth — the timing is brutal and beautiful: midnight set-alarm crowd on Netflix, and a 6 AM crowd on YouTube before work and college. Indian cities are among the top global sources for GTA content views, and the Nov 19 console launch (Rs pricing still unannounced for India) will make the pre-order window another search storm. Watch this page — we’ll update with the video embed and reactions as they land.

Bottom line: Set the alarm for 12:30 AM if you have Netflix — or sleep through and catch it free on YouTube from ~6:30 AM IST. Either way, tomorrow morning the entire internet will be arguing about the same few minutes of Leonida. November 19 is 84 days away.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does GTA 6 “An Extended Look” stream in India?

It premieres on Netflix at 12:30 AM IST on Friday, August 28 (3 p.m. ET, Thursday, August 27). The free YouTube release follows on Friday morning India time — IGN’s global listing shows a 9 a.m. Friday slot in one timezone, which lines up with roughly 6:30 AM IST, but confirm on Rockstar’s channel.

Is the GTA 6 Extended Look free to watch?

Yes — but timing differs. Netflix members can watch first (12:30 AM IST Friday). Everyone else can watch free on Rockstar’s official YouTube channel and the GTA VI site several hours later the same morning.

When does GTA 6 release?

November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. No PC version date has been announced — PC players will have to wait longer, as Mashable notes.

How long is the GTA 6 Extended Look?

Rockstar never officially confirmed the runtime before air. The Netflix premiere and YouTube release have now happened — watch the video on Rockstar’s channel for the full length, and see our update note above for what it revealed.
