Editorial Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

At Veritya Daily, we are committed to publishing original, accurate, and useful journalism. This Editorial Policy explains how we create, review, and maintain our content.

1. Our mission

To help readers understand the fast-moving worlds of AI, cryptocurrency, finance, cybersecurity, and technology — through original analysis, clear explanations, and honest reporting.

2. Authorship and expertise

Every article is attributed to a named author with relevant expertise. All authors have a public author page listing their credentials. We do not publish anonymous news content.

3. Sourcing and fact-checking

  • Primary sources first. We cite original announcements, official documents, regulatory filings, on-chain data, and named experts.
  • Every material claim is sourced.
  • We verify before publishing. Where facts cannot be verified, we say so explicitly.
  • We do not republish scraped or copied content.

4. Originality

All articles are original works created by Veritya Daily authors. We do not engage in "scaled content abuse." Where AI tools assist in drafting, this is disclosed and the content is edited, fact-checked, and reviewed by a human author.

5. Independence from advertisers and affiliates

Editorial content is independent of advertising and sponsorship. No advertiser influences our coverage. Sponsored content is always clearly labeled. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

6. Cryptocurrency and financial content (YMYL)

  • News and education only — never personalized financial advice.
  • Every crypto/finance article includes a clear "not financial advice" disclaimer.
  • We do not publish specific buy/sell calls paired with affiliate links.
  • Price predictions are clearly labeled as speculative estimates.

See our Financial Disclaimer.

7. Corrections

We acknowledge errors and correct them promptly. See our Corrections Policy.

8. Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to assist in research, drafting, or producing illustrations. AI never publishes autonomously. AI-assisted drafts are edited, fact-checked, and reviewed by a human author before publication.

9. Conflicts of interest

Authors disclose any material financial interest in the assets they cover. We do not accept payment for editorial coverage.

10. Content updates and freshness

Crypto/finance/AI content ages fast. We review and update top-performing articles quarterly. Updated articles display a visible "Updated [date]" stamp.

11. Contact

Editorial questions or story tips: tips@verityadaily.com or see our Contact page.
