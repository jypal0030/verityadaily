What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your actions and preferences over time.

Types of cookies we use

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Essential for the website to function. Enable core features like security and loading pages. No consent required.

2. Functional Cookies

Enable enhanced functionality (e.g., remembering your dark-mode preference). Consent required.

3. Analytics Cookies

Allow us to understand how visitors interact with the Site. Set by Google Analytics. Consent required.

4. Advertising Cookies

Used by Google AdSense to display and measure relevant ads. Consent required (blocked until you accept).

5. Third-Party / Affiliate Cookies

Some links are affiliate links. Destination services may set their own cookies. Consent required.
