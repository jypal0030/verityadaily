Q2 FY2027 Recap: $96.2B and a 106% Surge

NVIDIA's fiscal Q2 2027 (quarter ended July 26, reported August 26) wasn't just a beat - it was the largest quarterly revenue in the company's history and a near-doubling of the year-ago quarter:

The context matters as much as the numbers. Two quarters earlier, Q1 FY2027 came in at $81.6 billion (+85%) with Data Center at $75.2 billion (+92%) - and Q2's $96.2B landed well above the $91 billion guidance NVIDIA itself had issued in May. That gap between guide and actual is now a pattern: NVIDIA has beaten its own outlook in every quarter of this cycle.

Guidance: $108B for Q3, the 70% FY2028 Bombshell

The August 26 report did two unprecedented things at once. First, the near-term number: NVIDIA guided Q3 FY2027 revenue to $108 billion, plus or minus 2% - comfortably above the $104.19 billion consensus at the time (per Reuters). That's ~12% sequential growth from an already-record base, guided, not hoped.

Second, the history-maker: NVIDIA issued its first-ever year-ahead forecast - roughly 70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028. Press coverage called it a "growth bombshell meant to silence AI-bubble critics," and the market agreed: the stock climbed more than 4% in after-hours trading, and the Nasdaq rallied on the print the next day. For scale: CNBC's math puts fiscal 2027 consensus at ~$396 billion of revenue - so a 70% FY2028 implies a ~$673 billion annual run-rate, versus Morgan Stanley's more conservative 52% growth estimate.

NVDA Stock Forecast: $205 Range & the Road Ahead

As of mid-October, NVDA is consolidating near $205 after the post-earnings run - with Jensen Huang publicly framing a "$1 trillion Blackwell-to-Rubin vision" even as the broader chip sector wobbles. Wall Street's read after the latest tech rout: "just another scare" - the AI trade remains intact into year-end, with NVIDIA's guidance the anchor argument.

The honest forecast frame has three layers. Base case (guidance math): Q3 at $108B guided, Q4 implied higher - fiscal 2027 tracks toward the ~$396B consensus, which at 70% FY2028 growth builds the ~$673B scenario CNBC modeled. Bull catalysts: the Blackwell/Rubin super-cycle, sovereign-AI deals, and a guidance streak where every quarter's actual beats its own guide. Risk side: expectations inflation (the stock has averaged -2.79% the day after earnings and -5.31% over two days historically, per Motley Fool analysis), customer concentration (two customers were 36% of fiscal 2026 sales per Reuters), and any wobble in the $108B guide. None of this is investment advice - it's the scenario map the numbers draw.

What did Nvidia guide for Q3 FY2027?

What were Nvidia's Q2 FY2027 results?

What is Nvidia's FY2028 forecast?

CNBC's math puts the implied path at about $673 billion in revenue if fiscal 2027 lands at the ~$396 billion consensus, well above Morgan Stanley's 52% growth estimate.

Is Nvidia stock a buy after earnings?
