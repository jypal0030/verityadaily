iPhone 18 Release Date: Every Model, Price and Status (Updated)

By Jai October 17, 2026 | Updated regularly | Technology

The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max have been on sale since September 18, 2026 - and the foldable iPhone Duo arrives October 23 after pre-orders opened October 16. The surprise twist: the standard iPhone 18 isn't coming this year at all. Apple moved it to spring 2027, breaking its own September tradition. Here's every confirmed date, price and status - updated as things change.

Quick answer

Available now: iPhone 18 Pro ($1,199) and Pro Max ($1,299) - in stores since Sept 18, 2026. Pre-order open: iPhone Duo foldable ($1,999+) - ships Oct 23. Coming spring 2027: standard iPhone 18 (~$899 rumored) and iPhone 18e. Event was Sept 9, 2026.

iPhone Duo: the first foldable iPhone

September 9 wasn't just another Pro cycle - Apple entered the foldable race. The iPhone Duo opens to a 7.6-inch inner display, the largest ever on an iPhone, wrapped in what Reuters called a "passport-thin" closed profile:

  • Display: 7.6-inch foldable inner screen + cover display - the largest iPhone display ever.
  • Chip: A20 Pro (same as 18 Pro/Pro Max).
  • Camera: 48MP Dual Fusion system.
  • Storage: 256GB base up to a 2TB option - Apple's highest-capacity iPhone ever.
  • Colors: Star White and Night Sky (navy).
  • Price: from $1,999 (256GB) - carrier deals start around $57.99/month.

Mashable's launch-week check found the Duo backordered almost everywhere - if you want a 2026 delivery, pre-order timing matters more than usual. Carriers are sweetening it: T-Mobile and others run "on us" installment promos for trade-ins.

Why the base iPhone 18 moved to 2027

The strangest part of the 2026 cycle is also the most strategic. Multiple credible outlets - MacRumors, Macworld, PhoneArena, AppleInsider - converged on the same report: Apple is splitting its release calendar. Pro and premium models keep September; the standard iPhone 18 and 18e shift to spring 2027.

The logic reads three ways: it spreads supply-chain pressure across the year, gives each launch its own news cycle (no base model overshadowed by Pro Max reviews), and lets the base 18 ship with the same A20 generation rather than last year's chip. The "iPhone 18 in spring 2027" plan appeared in supply-chain reports as early as mid-2025 and has held firm through launch - this isn't a delay, it's the new calendar.

What that means practically: waiting for the base iPhone 18 means waiting roughly six months - and paying estimated $899 if the rumors hold. The iPhone 17 and 17e remain on sale at reduced prices in the meantime.

Is the iPhone 18 Pro good?

Short answer: yes, with a design twist. The 18 Pro pair adopts a unibody aluminum frame (a break from titanium) that CNET describes as more utilitarian, in four finishes - Black, Silver, Glacier, and the new deep Burgundy. Inside: A20 Pro on TSMC's 2nm process, an upgraded camera system, and the usual ProMax battery gains.

News-cycle check: launch coverage skewed positive on performance and cameras, with "very strong demand" reported by supply-chain watchers and the jailbreak community already cracking iOS 27.0.1 on the new hardware - the usual sign of brisk adoption. The main buyer hesitation is value: at $1,199 against a $1,999 foldable below it, some buyers are trading up to the Duo instead of down.

For the first time, "which iPhone should I get" has four answers depending on the month: Pro now, Duo this week, base next spring - or last year's iPhone 17 at a discount today.- Veritya Daily tech desk

Which iPhone 18 should you buy?

  • Want the best camera and performance today: iPhone 18 Pro - it's the completeness pick, available immediately.
  • Want maximum screen and the foldable future: iPhone Duo - accept the $1,999 entry and possible backorder wait.
  • Prefer smaller, cheaper, longer-waiting: standard iPhone 18 in spring 2027 - or the discounted iPhone 17 today.
  • Coming from Android: the Pro is the safest first-iPhone; check our Android vs iPhone breakdown before deciding.

How to pre-order the Duo (and actually get one)

  1. Order early in the window - pre-orders opened Oct 16 at 5 am PT; backorders built within hours.
  2. Pick your carrier path: Apple directly (unlocked), or carrier installments - T-Mobile and AT&T both run "on us"/credit promos with trade-ins.
  3. Storage matters: 256GB starts at $1,999; the 2TB option is the only way past 1TB on any iPhone.
  4. Watch the delivery estimate at checkout - launch-week orders may slip past Oct 23; in-store pickup at Apple Stores is the wildcard that sometimes beats shipping.

FAQ

Is an iPhone 18 going to come out?

It already did - twice. The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max launched September 18, 2026, and the iPhone Duo foldable arrives October 23. The standard iPhone 18 and 18e follow in spring 2027 under Apple's new split-release calendar.

Is the iPhone 18 launching in 2026?

Partly: Pro models and the foldable Duo launched in fall 2026. The base iPhone 18 and 18e will not launch in 2026 - Apple moved them to spring 2027, its first spring flagship cycle.

What date will the iPhone 18 be out?

Pro models: available since September 18, 2026. Duo: October 23, 2026. Standard iPhone 18: spring 2027 (expected, per MacRumors/Macworld/PhoneArena consensus - Apple hasn't announced the exact date).

Is the iPhone 18 Pro good?

Reviews position it as the most refined Pro yet - A20 Pro, upgraded cameras, new unibody aluminum design in four colors including Burgundy. Strong early demand and immediate iOS 27.0.1 jailbreak interest signal brisk adoption. $1,199 starting.

Should I wait for the iPhone 18 or buy iPhone 17 now?

If you want Pro or foldable: they're already out - no wait. If you want the base 18: that's spring 2027, and the discounted iPhone 17/17e is the rational holdover. Your budget and patience decide.
